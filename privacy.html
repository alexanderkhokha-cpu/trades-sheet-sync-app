Privacy policy: trades-sheet-sync

Last updated: 18 November 2026.

  • Who uses it: only the owner of this repository, on their own computer.
  • What it accesses: the Google Sheets scope (auth/spreadsheets), used to read and update one spreadsheet owned by the same person.
  • What it stores: an OAuth token on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any server run by this app; there is no such server.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.
  • Revoking access: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Contact: open an issue in this repository.
